## The Local/CI Divergence (Task 3)

The plan's checkpoint predicted this exact failure mode and instructed reporting the CI error text rather than adding a suppression. That is what happened.

1. `57c674b` marked QA-06 complete on the strength of a locally green typecheck.
2. CI then failed the `typecheck` job with **6 errors local had never produced**, all from `ultralytics` inference. `255807c` **un-marked QA-06** rather than leaving a claim standing that CI contradicted.
3. Diagnosis: `h5py` and `ultralytics` ship no `py.typed`, so basedpyright infers from library source — a different resolved version infers differently. CI resolved basedpyright `1.39.10` against local `1.38.4`, and `ultralytics` `8.4.138`, which changed `Model.predict()` annotations to include `Iterator[...]`.
4. `ddb8eb2` pinned `basedpyright==1.38.4` exactly (it *is* the gate; a floating gate means local and CI disagree about what "0 errors" means) and bounded `ultralytics>=8.0,<=8.4.28` (bounded rather than pinned, because unlike the dev-only checker it ships to users). Both carry inline comments explaining the reasoning and the condition for raising them.
5. Run `33703999565` on `2c30f5c`: **`typecheck` green in 45s**, all 8 jobs green. ROADMAP criterion 2 satisfied — the job has now passed on `dev`.

Zero suppression directives were added at any point in this sequence. The suppression count is 73 both before and after the divergence was resolved.
