<objective>
Close the four one-off rule violations that are unrelated to the h5py cluster and unrelated
to each other: a `str` crossing a `Literal` boundary at the click seam, a duplicated
annotation inside one function, and a nested-dict value that does not fit the declared type
of the field it is stored in.

Purpose: the last three of these are trivially fixable and the fourth is a trap. Widening
`camera_visibility` to admit its nested `distribution` key is the obvious move and it
**creates four new errors** in `evaluation/output.py`, because that consumer calls `float()`
and `int()` on sibling keys. This plan isolates that trap so it is met deliberately.
Output: four files at 0 errors, and `evaluation/output.py` still at 0.
</objective>
