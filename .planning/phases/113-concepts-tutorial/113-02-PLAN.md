At both angular-error sites in `tests/unit/calibration/test_luts.py`, replace the
`torch.acos` of a clamped float32 dot product with a numerically stable angle
between two vectors, computed in float64:

- cast both `lut_dirs` and `model_dirs` to float64 before measuring;
- compute the cross-product norm along the last dimension with
  `torch.linalg.cross(..., dim=-1).norm(dim=-1)`;
- compute the dot product along the last dimension;
- take the angle as `torch.atan2(cross_norm, dot)`, then convert to degrees by
  multiplying by `180.0 / torch.pi`, keeping the existing `.abs()`.

The clamp to `[-1, 1]` becomes unnecessary and should be removed along with the
`acos` call, because `atan2` is well-conditioned across the whole range.

**Keep both thresholds exactly as they are: `0.01` at the first site and `0.1` at
the second.** This is a correction to a broken metric, not a tolerance
relaxation — with the stable formula the real margin becomes roughly 500x. Do not
widen, skip, xfail, or otherwise weaken either assertion (see this plan's
`must_haves.prohibitions`). Keep the assertion messages' meaning intact,
including the threshold values they quote.

Leave every other part of both tests untouched: the seeds, the sample counts and
ranges, the `image_size`, the `grid_step`, the `torch.no_grad()` blocks, the
`.cpu()` calls, and both origin-distance assertions.

Before committing, prove the assertion is not insensitive: temporarily perturb
one direction vector by a fraction of a degree, confirm the test goes red, then
revert the perturbation and confirm it goes green again. Record both observed
values in the SUMMARY. This is the check that would have caught the vacuous pass
D-17 describes.
